Privacy policy

Your saved trips stay on your device. No accounts. No ads.

For the ilovetrains website, web app, iPhone app and Android app.
Updated 9 September 2026.

On your device

Your saved trips, history, preferences and offline timetables stay on your device. The app uses them to suggest your next trip and the way home.

If you allow location access, the app uses it to find a nearby station. Your location and travel updates are handled on your device. Your location, saved-trip list and history aren't uploaded.

When you connect

For online departures, the app sends the stations and requested time to our server. It gets results from Transport for NSW and briefly caches them, without creating a personal trip profile. Connections use HTTPS. Our hosting providers, including Cloudflare, receive your IP address and request details and may keep access logs.

The web app sends anonymous usage counts to improve suggestions and compare interface changes. They contain no user or device identifier, trip details or location. The service records when counts arrive. Your browser's Do Not Track setting stops these counts.

This website has no analytics scripts or advertising cookies. It checks your device type in your browser to choose a download button, without storing a profile or sending an extra request. Your data is never sold.

Messages you send

In-app feedback sends your message, its category and the app name. It doesn't attach your location, trips or device identifiers. The service records when the message arrives. Feedback is sent only when you submit it, including when Do Not Track is enabled.

If you email support, we receive your email address and message. We use feedback and support messages to help you and improve the app. Feedback stays stored until deleted; it has no automatic expiry. You can request deletion by email. Leave out personal details that aren't needed.

Your choices and contact

You can turn off location access in your device settings. Uninstalling the native app, or clearing the web app's site data, removes its local data. Saved trips have no cloud backup and can't be recovered afterwards.

For a privacy question or to request deletion of a message, Email about privacy. Include enough of the message to identify it.
