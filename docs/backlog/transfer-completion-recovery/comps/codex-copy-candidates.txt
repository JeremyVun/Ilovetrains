A. THE ONWARD CLAUSE

THEN - Plain sequence
AND THEN - Explicitly connects the next leg
AFTER THAT - Makes the separation clearest
NEXT - Matches the compact rail labels
THEN T4 - Names the train that follows

B. THE CHANGE LABEL WITH A TIME

TOWN HALL · BOARD 10:08 - Makes the boarding time explicit
TOWN HALL · BOARD AT 10:08 - Fuller instruction; exactly 26 characters
TOWN HALL · T4 10:08 - Identifies the service by line and time
TOWN HALL · NEXT T4 10:08 - Explicitly names the next service
TOWN HALL · DEPARTS 10:08 - Distinguishes departure from arrival

C. THE MARK WHERE THE MISSED TRAIN LEFT

09:58 LEFT - Identifies the departure and says what happened
T4 LEFT - Names the train; the notch supplies the time
T4 DEPARTED - More formal, still explicit
09:58 T4 - Identifies the exact service; the notch conveys departure
09:58 TRAIN - Names the service by time; the notch conveys departure

D. THE STATUS LINE CARRYING BOTH FACTS

LATE · CONNECTION MISSED - Both facts; 24 characters
LATE · CONNECTION GONE - Both facts; 22 characters
RUNNING LATE · T4 LEFT - Keeps the existing status; the notch identifies which T4
9 MIN LATE · 09:58 LEFT - Gives the delay and identifies the lost departure
CONNECTION MISSED - Drops lateness because the missed connection changes what you do next
