# On the train, and the change just got tight

**Persona.** Priya, focused on the 09:24 Rhodes → Bondi Junction. Change at
Town Hall from Platform 3 to Platform 5.

**Situation.** 09:40, on the T9. The train is now running four minutes late.
The connection was seven minutes; it is now three. She has her bag on her
lap and one hand free.

**Goal.** Hard: "will I make the change, where do I run to, and what do I do
if I miss it?"

## What happens

1. She opens the app. The header reads `RUNNING LATE` in the warning colour.
2. The figure counts `TO CHANGE`. The dwell segment on the axis is painted in
   the warning colour.
3. The instruction line says `Tight change · 3 min · Platform 5`.
4. She stands up two stops early and positions herself by the doors.

## Success looks like

- The warning is unmissable: colour on the axis, warning word, the minutes.
- Both platforms are readable: the one she gets off at and the one she runs
  to.
- She knows what happens if she misses it.

## Pressure points

- Before departure the header does not paint the tight change at all. The
  board row and journey detail do. A rider who checked at home saw a normal
  change and is surprised on the train. The dwell segment must be painted in
  every phase, not only after boarding.
- The tight-change instruction replaces `Get off at Town Hall · Platform 3`
  with `Tight change · 3 min · Platform 5`. The platform she must *get off
  at* is gone from the sentence. Only the pin on the axis carries it.
- "What if I miss it" has no answer on screen. The next T4 from Platform 5 is
  not named anywhere. Does she have to go back to the board and read the
  next row's change time?
- The delay claim needs fresh data. In a tunnel with a stale board, the
  status drops to `RUNNING` and the tight colour may vanish exactly when she
  needs it.
