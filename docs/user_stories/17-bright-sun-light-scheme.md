# Bright sun on the platform

**Persona.** Dev at Harris Park, 08:05, phone in light mode, sun behind.

**Situation.** Squinting. Screen brightness at max. One glance to check the
platform and headsign.

**Goal.** Hard: "platform and headsign, now."

## What happens

1. Dev opens the app. The light scheme prints on warm paper.
2. The platform cap is filled in the line colour with paper numerals. For a
   T1 service that is paper on yellow, 2:1 contrast by design.
3. The headsign is 15px, 300 weight, secondary ink.

## Success looks like

- Platform number legible at arm's length in sunlight.
- Headsign legible without bringing the phone closer.

## Pressure points

- The owner accepted paper-on-yellow for T1 identity. In sunlight that
  exception is the number Dev needs most. Is a 2:1 numeral readable at
  arm's length outdoors, or only at a desk?
- The 250-weight figure and 300-weight headsign are elegant indoors. Thin
  type is the first thing sunlight erases.
- Light mode is derived by measurement, not by outdoor testing. This story
  is the outdoor test.
