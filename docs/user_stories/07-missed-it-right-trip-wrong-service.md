# Missed the 09:24, caught the 09:39

**Persona.** Priya, focused on the 09:24 at home, then delayed by a phone
call.

**Situation.** 09:41, on the 09:39 instead. The app still believes she is
on the 09:24, which left seventeen minutes ago.

**Goal.** Fuzzy: "just show me my actual train."

## What happens

1. She opens the app. The header is directions for the 09:24: the marker is
   two thirds along, `TO CHANGE` counts down to a Town Hall arrival she will
   not make.
2. There is no unfocus. She taps the trip row for the board.
3. The 09:24 has gone from the board's future; the 09:39 is now `Now` or
   just departed. She opens its detail and taps `Take this train`.
4. Home becomes directions for the 09:39.

## Success looks like

- Two taps from wrong train to right train, no confession, no settings.
- The old ride is not recorded as completed.

## Pressure points

- The 09:39 has *departed* by the time she looks. Departed services drop off
  the board's future and are only in the past register if a past page covers
  them. Can she find the 09:39 at all, one minute after it left?
- If she cannot, the only remaining path is waiting for the 09:24's focus to
  expire thirty minutes after its scheduled arrival.
- The completed-ride record for the 09:24 will still be written when its
  arrival passes, so her ride history gains a ride she was not on. Home
  inference and "last ridden" are fed from it.
- The project accepts this gap "until it bites". This story is what biting
  looks like: every instruction on screen is for the wrong train.
